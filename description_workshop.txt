Pick [b]Wallpaper Engine[/b] at the bottom of the title screen, same as any other theme. It boots looking like normal tML. The wrench sits between the menu buttons and Switch Menu Theme — that's the whole UI.

Logo and wallpaper are two different slots. You can grab a sky from one mod and a logo from another. I keep having to say this.

[h2]Stuff you can do[/h2]
[list]
[*] Your own PNG/JPG wallpapers and logos
[*] Skies and logos from other loaded menu themes (Calamity, Infernum, Fables, Wrath of the Gods, Catalyst, whatever else you have on)
[*] Layers on top: stars, fog, rain, beat pulse, etc.
[*] Music player — ogg / mp3 / wav, shuffle, loop, delete a track if you hate it
[*] Clock, greeting, moon, Discord widget (paste a server ID, Join opens the Discord app)
[*] Drag / scale / hide the vanilla title-screen junk
[*] Recolor the tModLoader window, circle or bar for the wrench menu
[*] Dump a TTF/OTF on the menu buttons if vanilla text is boring
[*] Save the whole look and load it later (Client → Looks)
[/list]

Borrowed skies are the other menu's actual background, not a screenshot. Same idea for logos. Wallpaper Engine stays selected; I never leave their theme as the current menu. If their menu is locked behind a boss, it won't show up yet. If it fails to draw, I skip it and go back to vanilla.

Don't dump other mods' crashes in the comments. I can't fix Calamity for you.

Client-side.
