A fully customizable tModLoader title theme. Starts as vanilla tML — open the wrench to change wallpapers, music, logo, widgets, layout, and the Windows window chrome. Logo and sky from other mods mix independently. Custom tracks can be deleted from the music panel. Optional Discord server widget: paste the server ID in the widgets panel (enable Server Widget in Discord). The widget refreshes on its own, shows who is in voice, and Join Voice opens the Discord app when it is installed.
