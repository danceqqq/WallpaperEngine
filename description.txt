A fully customizable tModLoader title theme. Starts as vanilla tML — open the wrench to change wallpapers, music, logo, widgets, layout, and the Windows window chrome. Logo and sky from other mods mix independently. Custom tracks can be deleted from the music panel. Optional Discord server widget with compact / banner / member-list styles, live voice channels, and Join that opens the Discord app. Widget tiles in the panel show live previews; hover an active tile for its settings.
